Aggelos Bletsas (Fellow, IEEE) received the diploma (Hons.) degree in electrical and computer engineering from Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece, in 1998, and the M.Sc. and Ph.D. degrees from the Media Arts and Sciences (Media Lab), Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT), Cambridge, MA, USA, in 2001 and 2005, respectively. He has worked with Mitsubishi Electric Research Laboratories (MERL), Cambridge, MA, USA, and the RadioCommunications Laboratory (RCL), Department of Physics, Aristotle University of Thessaloniki, Greece. From 2009 to 2023, he served as a Faculty, starting as an Assistant Professor in 2009, continuing as an Associate Professor in 2014 and a Full Professor in 2018, with the School of Electrical and Computer Engineering, Technical University of Crete, Greece. In 2024, he joined as a Full Professor with WINLAB and the Department of ECE, Rutgers University. His research interests include scalable wireless communications and sensor networking, with emphasis on ultra-low power/cost environmental sensing and precision agriculture, backscatter radio and RFID, wireless localization and ambiently-powered inference networks.

Prof. Bletsas received the 2012–2013 Technical University of Crete Research Excellence Award. He was co-recipient of the IEEE Communications Society 2008 Marconi Prize Paper Award in Wireless Communications, and various Best (Student) Paper Awards and distinctions, such as in IEEE RFID-TA 2011, IEEE ICASSP 2015, IEEE RFID-TA 2017, MOCAST 2018, IEEE WCNEE 2021 and IEEE RFID 2023. In December 2020, his students won the Second Prize in the 2020 IEEE ComSoc “Communications Technology Changing the World” Student Competition. He is also proud of his students who were winners for two consecutive years of the 2009–2011 and 2011–2012 best Diploma Thesis contest among all Greek Universities on “Advanced Wireless Systems,” awarded by IEEE VTS/AES joint Greek Chapter. One of his articles is ranked 1st in Google Scholar Classic Papers in Computer Networks and Wireless Communication. He has served as a Technical Program Committee Member for major IEEE conferences and as an Associate Editor for the IEEE TRANSACTIONS ON WIRELESS COMMUNICATIONS from 2015 to 2021 and IEEE WIRELESS COMMUNICATIONS LETTERS from 2011 to 2016. Since June 2022, he has been serving as an Area Editor for the IEEE TRANSACTIONS ON WIRELESS COMMUNICATIONS
